The problem

Warranties expire quietly. Repairs don't.

Most people own dozens of things under warranty and can't name three of them. The receipt is in a drawer, the purchase date is a guess, and the terms are a PDF nobody has ever opened.

The drawer

Receipts fade, get thrown out, or live in an inbox you'll never search successfully.

The gap

Cover ends without a word. You find out at the counter, holding something broken.

The terms

Even in warranty, nobody knows whether this particular fault is actually covered.

The number

And when you do call, it's the importer who honours it — not the brand on the box.

01 · Capture

Get it in, in seconds.

The fastest way to lose a user is to make the first product feel like paperwork. Four ways in, and only three fields are ever required: what it is, what kind of thing it is, and when you bought it.

Scan a receipt

Point the camera. We read the rest.

Dates, totals, currency and invoice numbers are pulled out by pattern before any AI is involved — faster, cheaper and more reliable. The product name and brand go to a model only when the receipt doesn't give them up directly.

Then you get a review screen. Every extracted value is shown with what we read, and anything we're unsure about is flagged. Nothing is saved until you say so.

Receipts, invoices, warranty certificates and service records — PDF, JPEG, PNG or HEIC, attached to the product they belong to.

Find it the way you'd describe it

Search by name, brand, model or the serial number you're reading off the back of the appliance. Filter by status, category or purchase date.

Duplicates flagged, never deleted

Upload the same receipt twice and we'll say so. Two identical products is a real thing people own — we warn rather than decide for you.

02 · Know

Understand it, don't just store it.

A folder of receipts is filing. Knowing that your washing machine has 312 days left, who honours the warranty in your country, and what the terms actually exclude — that's the product.

Warranty status at a glance

Active, ending soon or expired — with the exact end date, days remaining and a timeline from purchase to expiry. Extended cover adds on top.

Always sourced

Anything we worked out rather than you typed carries where it came from and how sure we are. Low confidence asks you to confirm before we treat it as settled.

The right company, not the famous one

Manufacturer, importer, retailer and repairer are four different businesses. We keep them apart, so you call whoever actually honours the claim where you live.

Automatic detection

We'll fill in the warranty — and show our working.

Match the brand, model and country against the warranty database and the duration, provider and terms come back filled in. What you also get is the receipt for that claim: which document it came from, when it was last checked, and how confident we are.

And when we don't know, we say we don't know. There is no default twelve months. A plausible guess that turns out wrong when you try to claim is the worst thing this app could do to you.

Coverage check

"Is this actually covered?"

Describe the fault in your own words. We find the clauses in the warranty that bear on it and give you a structured answer: how likely it is to be covered, why, what the exclusions are, and what to do next.

The strongest thing we will ever say is likely covered — and it has to cite a real clause from a real document, quoted word for word, which you can read yourself. We're not the warranty provider and we won't pretend to speak for one.

How we build it

Four rules we don't break.

This app's only real asset is whether you can trust what it tells you. These are the constraints that protect that, written into the code rather than the marketing.

We never invent a warranty

If we don't know the duration, the status is unknown and we ask you. There is no default twelve months anywhere in the system. A plausible guess that fails you at the repair counter is worse than an honest blank.

We always show our sources

Anything derived rather than typed carries where it came from and how sure we are, right next to the value. If we're not confident, we say so before you rely on it.

We never say “covered”

We're not the warranty provider and we can't commit anyone to paying a claim. The strongest thing we'll say is likely covered, backed by a clause you can read for yourself.

We never hold your data hostage

Cancel and you keep everything: read it, edit it, export it, delete it. A lapsed subscription stops you adding new products. That's all it does.
